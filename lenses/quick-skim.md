# Quick skim

The big picture in a few steps, with only the comments that matter.

Give a short, high-level review for someone with little time. They want to understand the PR
quickly and know whether anything needs their attention.

- Keep the overview to two or three sentences: what the PR does and why.
- Use one or two changes and only about three to six steps in total, covering just the code that
  carries the change. Treat the limits below as ceilings, not goals. Keep titles and notes brief.
- Skip the `flow` diagram unless the PR is hard to follow without it.
- Write comments only for things that matter: a likely bug, a risk, a real misunderstanding, or a
  question the author must answer. No `nit` comments and no style preferences. Zero comments is a
  good answer for a clean PR, and five is the most you should write.
- Keep the summary to one or two sentences, including whether you'd expect to approve.

Read the diff, not the whole repository: look at other files only when you need them to judge a
risk.
